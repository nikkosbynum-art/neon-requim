## Core loop
Six unrestricted deployed recruits, automatic combat and skills, dynamic formation with optional out-of-combat position preferences. Squad advances, fights escalating waves, earns credits, recruits and levels units, farms highest cleared holding wave after defeat, and manually performs System Reboot for permanent advancement.
## Districts
Undercity 1–25; Neon Row 26–50; Black Grid 51–75; Enforcement Zone 76–100; The Spire 101–125; procedural endless after.
## Factions
Chrome Saints; Ghost Circuit; Redline Syndicate; Vanguard Zero; Helix Dominion; The Fractured. Independents possible.
## Recruitment
No gacha. Standard recruits purchased using earned credits. Named unique boss defeated = guaranteed unlock; once recruited, named enemy never spawns again (generic archetypes may).
## Approved gameplay decisions
1. Hybrid advancing battlefield with camera-following parallax and loose encounter battle lines (C).
2. Dynamic formation, optional player position/role preferences, no class-locked slots (C).
3. Fully automatic targeting and abilities; no manual triggers/target overrides (A).
4. Hybrid power-driven combat: relative squad power sets expected advantage, stats/abilities/synergies resolve actual battle (C).
5. Immediate retreat after defeat to highest previously cleared holding wave, automatic farming, no recovery timer; stalemate cinematic once on arrival (A).
6. Individual run-levels and full-cost bulk upgrade actions (C).
7. Exponential wave credit rewards and leveling costs, with milestone/research/reboot acceleration; formulas open (C).
8. Reboot yields lifetime and spendable Network Influence. Lifetime total grants passive benefit; spending reduces only spendable balance. Four research branches: Combat Protocols, Underground Economy, Network Infrastructure, Personnel Development (C).
9. Manual reboot default; future unlockable opt-in automation, never automatic Influence spending (C).
10. Offline earns credits only based on saved holding wave; no offline waves, bosses, leveling or reboot. Eight-hour cap is target (A).
11. Four permanent character evolution tiers with stats and visuals; selected milestones modify abilities/add passives; separate cyberware specialization (C).
## Reboot
Resets credits and individual run-levels. Preserves recruit unlocks, evolution, lifetime/spendable Influence, research and opt-in settings. First eligibility target wave 25 is not locked.
## Evolution
Street Grade, Combat Grade, Military Grade, Apex Grade. Signature silhouettes remain recognizable. Evolution unlock conditions/materials not approved.
## First playable target
One district, 12 recruit definitions, 25 waves, three enemy factions, research/reboot/save eventually. First vertical slice should start smaller: BREAKER Tier I, one enemy, one encounter zone, auto-combat, credits, leveling, save.
## Open details
Damage formulas, effective power formula, speed/cooldowns, scaling, exact boss logic, safe wave definition, retry progression timing, offline cap final balance, cyberware costs, evolution unlocks.
