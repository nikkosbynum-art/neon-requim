# BREAKER Character & Sprite Specification

Source: https://app.notion.com/p/40394c5658c881edb7fae758125174f1

**Migration note:** Locally reconstructed working copy based on connected Notion reads and project decisions; not a verbatim Notion export. Consult canonical page for complete original text.

Official visual direction: **C — Underground War Machine**. Former prizefighter with damaged neural regulator, scarred human face, messy dark undercut, boxer stance, giant asymmetric RIGHT mechanical fist, matte black/gunmetal/crimson. Keep face and asymmetry recognizable.
Evolution: Streetbreaker → Combat Conversion → Siege Conversion → War Machine.
Modular components: head, torso, upper/lower arms, giant right fist, smaller left fist, hips, upper/lower legs, regulator, attachments and VFX. Stable pivot points and compatible swappable tiers.
Approved implementation #1: Hybrid modular Godot 4 2D skeletal animation with hand-authored specialized combat poses and VFX. Tier I prototype idle, walk, punch, hit, defeat; verify facing, grounded feet, aligned pivots, impact sync and Tier II art-swap feasibility.
Concept art is NOT production-ready cutout sprites. Refer to `drive_assets/Breaker_Tier1_Godot_Production_Spec.md` for the detailed existing spec and four PNG boards.
