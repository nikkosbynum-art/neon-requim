## Review caveats

The user approved the concept designs for use as visual references. Details introduced by the concepts (faces, clothing, proportions, hairstyles, faction-color assignments, gear construction, gender presentation where the roster was silent, and cyberware placement) are now part of the approved visual direction for these Tier I references. The approval does not verify hidden anatomy, establish part boundaries/pivots, or make these images production sprite assets. RECOIL's prosthetic side is now fixed by the approved concept image for visual direction; validate that side consistently in the source sheet and rig specification.

The board is a quick identity/silhouette discussion aid. Although its contrast and palette fit the cyberpunk direction, its rendering is more polished and detailed than the target in-game side-view sprite scale. It has not been sliced, measured for proportions, or tested for readable animation. Do not import it into runtime scenes or create character-art manifests from its panels.

## Next art-production gate

The individual Tier I visual directions are approved. The new anatomy turnarounds are a separate review gate: compare each against its approved concept and correct any drift. Then author transparent parts and pivots and complete character-specific rig, seam, and animation reviews. Visual similarity, anatomy, joints, and animation must be reviewed per character; one board-wide test cannot establish those properties.
