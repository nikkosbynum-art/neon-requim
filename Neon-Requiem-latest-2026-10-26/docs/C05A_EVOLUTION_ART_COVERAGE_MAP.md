## Evidence required for every tier entry

Before a tier state can be called production-ready, its evidence packet must include:

1. An owner-approved, character-specific stage brief tied to the preceding stage and stable identity anchors. It must say what visible art changes at this step; no generic armor, weapon, cyberware, or color treatment is inferred here.
2. Source-aligned, named editable layers or a reviewed semantic boundary plan for every affected part, with ownership, overlap/draw order, provenance, source coordinates, and inferred hidden regions clearly labeled.
3. A native-scale checkerboard contact sheet and source ownership/boundary overlay. Review each part at 100%; automated alpha or pixel matches do not pass semantic completeness.
4. A character/tier manifest with dimensions, authored pivots, layer order, provenance, production status, and runtime status. A provenance mismatch blocks reliance on that workspace until exact source evidence resolves it.
5. A neutral assembly against the approved views, then representative authored idle/walk/attack/hit/defeat poses as applicable. Capture the actual combined pose at the guide's 1280×720 and 800×450 review sizes and record silhouette, role/gear readability, seams, grounding, and deformation.
6. Rig compatibility evidence: stable skeleton, pivots, facing, and ground anchor across the tier set where anatomy supports reuse; explicit character-specific rig exceptions where it does not. Demonstrate the art swap and required animation/impact/effect behavior only after the art passes earlier gates.

Candidate status remains quarantined until visual, semantic, provenance, pivot, deformation, rig/animation, and production gates pass for that entry. The all-twelve scope does not approve tier stat/trait changes or new gameplay behavior.
