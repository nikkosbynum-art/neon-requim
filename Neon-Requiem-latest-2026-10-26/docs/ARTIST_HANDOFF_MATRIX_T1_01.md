## Priority and packet key

- **P0 — source decision:** resolve identity, count, or carry-state conflicts before labeling or making parts.
- **P1 — semantic art:** provide source-backed part layers or boundary maps; this unlocks native-scale semantic review.
- **P2 — attachment/rig:** provide authored pivots, endpoints, and pose overlap maps after the relevant parts exist; this unlocks a character-specific rig preview.
- **T — tooling:** resolve an application/workflow blocker; this is not an artist request.

Repeated requests are deduplicated by packet type: **CANON** resolves source/roster identity; **LAYER** supplies transparent named art or a marked boundary/ownership overlay; **ATTACH** maps grips, mounts, or tether endpoints; **PIVOT** records artist-placed joint/attachment points. Character-specific content remains explicit below.
