# Founding Recruit Cards — Content Draft 01

**Status:** Source-grounded content draft; not final card art, a combat specification, or a canon change.

## Source and card rules

- Roster facts and biographies are paraphrased from the live [02 — Founding Recruit Roster](https://app.notion.com/p/40394c5658c8812d849be3d32629920e), fetched read-only on 2026-10-26 at 03:12:07 UTC. Notion returned `verification.state=unverified`.
- The visual links point to approved Tier I concept and anatomy references indexed in [`TIER_I_APPROVED_VISUAL_SOURCE_INDEX_01.md`](TIER_I_APPROVED_VISUAL_SOURCE_INDEX_01.md). They guide appearance; they are not finished card illustrations or production sprites.
- The roster gives one signature-skill summary per recruit. All twelve remain **SUMMARY ONLY — NOT EXECUTABLE** in [`I-04C_ABILITY_CONTRACT_DEFINITION_PACKET.md`](I-04C_ABILITY_CONTRACT_DEFINITION_PACKET.md).
- No source located in this pass specifies a canonical basic attack for any recruit. Keep that card field marked **OPEN**. Do not infer a weapon, move, target, cadence, or effect from class labels or reference poses.
- Some roster signature skills heal, buff, suppress, create decoys, or alter targeting. The card field is therefore called **Signature Ability / Alternate Action**, not “alternate attack,” until the design owner decides whether all abilities should use that label.
